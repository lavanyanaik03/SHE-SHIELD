SHE-SHIELD is a hackathon prototype built for SIH 2026, problem statement SIH26093 (Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment). AI features provide a risk/vulnerability assessment and support recommendation only — not a medical diagnosis. All data in this demo is stored locally in your browser and is not transmitted anywhere without your explicit action.
